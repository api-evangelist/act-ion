# ACT-ion Battery Technologies

> ACT-ion Battery Technologies, Inc. is an advanced battery materials company founded in
> Dallas, Texas and spun out of Hunt Energy Enterprises, operating an advanced cathode pilot
> plant in Carrollton, Texas. It manufactures surface-engineered single-crystal cathode active
> materials (CAM) for lithium-ion batteries — LFP, LMFP, NMC and lithium-manganese-rich (LMR)
> chemistries, marketed as ACT-ion LXP+ — using a chemistry-agnostic high-throughput continuous
> synthesis process that deposits a multi-functional protective and conductive shell during
> particle growth.
